Python to ML in 100 Days

A five-level path from your first print() to training and serving a machine-learning model. Each day teaches one idea, practises it, then uses it in a small project you publish.

How every day runs

Learn→Practice→Build→Test→Understand→Publish

Days are a roadmap, not a deadline. If a topic needs more practice, it gets a second day. Capstone days close each level by combining everything in it. Click any project to open its lesson, exercises, project code and tests. Items marked suggested come from the part of your brief that was cut off, so they are my proposal until you confirm them.
